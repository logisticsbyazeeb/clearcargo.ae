FAQ

Common questions

How fast will I get a quote?

Usually within one working day. Urgent air shipments: mark it in the form or message us on WhatsApp.

Do you ship to India?

The UAE–India corridor, starting with Vizhinjam International Seaport in Kerala, is our first planned expansion. Send us your shipment details and we will tell you what is available now.

Where are you based?

ClearCargo DWC LLC is in the Dubai South Free Zone, with access to the Jebel Ali Free Zone for sea shipping and Al Maktoum International Airport for air cargo.

Do you handle customs clearance on its own?

Yes. You can use clearance without our freight service. Send us the commercial invoice, packing list and bill of lading or airway bill.

Can you ship dangerous or restricted goods?

Some, with the right permits and packaging. Tell us what the cargo is when you request a quote and we will confirm what is possible.

How do I follow my shipment?

Use the tracking box at the top of the page, or reply to any of our update emails and your coordinator will answer.

Which payment terms do you offer?

Terms depend on the account. Ask in your quote request and we will confirm.
